Who We Are

A Town Shaped by Heritage and Community

Amanokrom is a historic town in the Akuapem area of Ghana's Eastern Region, known for its cultural importance, traditional leadership and place within the wider story of Okuapeman.

Set on the Akuapem Ridge, the town combines scenic beauty with rich social memory. Its history, customs and community life connect present generations with the experiences and traditions of their ancestors.

Its people are known for resilience, hospitality, reverence for tradition and a firm commitment to communal progress. These qualities continue to shape the character of Amanokrom and its hopes for the future.

Our Identity

Tradition, Service and Responsibility

As the seat of the Gyasehene within the Akuapem Traditional Area, Amanokrom occupies a unique place in the political and cultural life of the ridge.

The town's identity is inseparable from the values of service, courage, dignity, loyalty and disciplined self-development.

These values provide a foundation for community life, respect for traditional institutions and the collective responsibility to preserve our heritage while working towards a stronger future.

What Defines Us

The Values of Amanokrom

Our identity is reflected in the principles we honour, the way we treat one another and the future we seek to build together.

Service

We value service to our community and respect the responsibilities that come with leadership, citizenship and belonging.

Courage

We honour the courage and resilience that have helped shape our history and continue to inspire our community.

Dignity

We uphold the dignity of our people, our customs and our traditional institutions.

Loyalty

We cherish loyalty to our community, respect for our heritage and a shared commitment to unity.

Tradition

We preserve the stories, customs and cultural knowledge passed down through generations.

Self-Development

We encourage discipline, learning, personal growth and collective progress for a stronger community.

Our Past • Our Present • Our Future

Preserving Our Heritage for Generations

Amanokrom's story belongs to its people. Through respect for our traditions, commitment to our community and pride in our identity, we continue to honour the past while building towards a brighter future.
