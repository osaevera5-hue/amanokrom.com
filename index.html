WELCOME TO AMANOKROM

A Proud Community, A Lasting Legacy

Amanokrom is a proud Akuapem community with a rich cultural heritage, a distinguished traditional leadership and a history that continues to shape the identity of its people.

Our traditions, festivals, customs and community spirit connect generations, preserving our heritage while inspiring a brighter future.
